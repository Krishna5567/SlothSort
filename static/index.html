Why Open Innovation Matters for SlothSort

The core promise of the "Slow and Steady" challenge is that the open pieces must be what make your project work. Here is how SlothSort leverages open-source AI to achieve what closed proprietary models cannot:

Challenge Dimension 🌿 Open-Source AI (SlothSort) ☁️ Proprietary Cloud APIs

🦥 The Sloth Philosophy: Zero Maintenance, Infinite Longevity

Closed APIs deprecate models every 6 months, enforce token rate limits, require billing credit cards, and disconnect if your Wi-Fi drops. SlothSort runs open weights (SmolLM2-360M + Whisper + pyttsx3). Once installed, it works forever—on an airplane, during a power outage, or at 3 AM with zero cost and complete peace of mind.
